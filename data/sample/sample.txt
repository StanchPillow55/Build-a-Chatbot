Meetings
The AI & ML Club meets every Thursday at 6:00 PM in the Student Union,
Room 210. Meetings are open to all San Jose State University students
regardless of major or prior experience.

Membership
Students join the club by filling out the sign-up form at the first general
meeting of the semester and joining the club Discord server. There is no
membership fee for the Fall 2025 semester.

Officers
The club is led by a President, a Vice President, a Treasurer, and an Events
Coordinator. Officer elections are held at the end of the Spring semester for
the following academic year.

Events
During the Fall 2025 semester the club runs a weekly workshop series covering
Python for machine learning, building neural networks with PyTorch, and an
introduction to retrieval-augmented generation. The club also hosts one guest
speaker night per month.

Projects
Members can join project teams that build small applications. Example project
areas include computer vision demos, chatbots, and data-visualization
dashboards. Project teams present their work at the end-of-semester showcase.
